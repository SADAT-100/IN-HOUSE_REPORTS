      How each number is calculated

      Net sales
      Noon net proceeds of items that are Delivered, Shipped, Processing or Pending. Returned, failed and cancelled items are left out.
      Orders
      Noon order numbers with at least one item that was not cancelled.
      Noon view
      Referral, shipping and order subsidies exactly as Noon charged them (the first set of fee columns). Revenue and profit use the workbook's Revenue and Profit columns.
      In House view
      Your own fee figures (Comm, Shipping and the second subsidy column). Revenue and profit use the Revenue2 and Profit2 columns. The two views are the same until June 2026.
      Profit
      Revenue minus the unit cost of each item sold. Returned items carry their fees but no product cost.
      Profit after account charges
      Profit plus ads, ad subsidies, FBN charges and damaged-return credits from the All Fee tab. Shown for whole months and for all delivery types only, because these charges are not split by order.
      Payouts
      Transfers listed in the Statement & Payment tab. Statement totals in the PS- tab already include VAT.
      Period filter
      Order money follows the order date. The range buttons count back from the last order in the data, not from today.
